The short version

  • Your breath stays on your phone. The microphone is analysed on the device, in real time. Audio is never recorded, stored, logged or sent anywhere.
  • No account is needed to play.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not build advertising profiles ourselves.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting SDK. The game sends us no gameplay statistics. If your phone shares usage & diagnostics data with app developers, Google Play may give us anonymous crash reports.
  • Ads are optional. There are no banners and no interstitials; a rewarded ad only plays when you choose it. The ads SDK (Google AdMob) loads an ad in the background so it is ready if you want it. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland ads are never personalised.
  • You can delete your data from the game at any time: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data.

Microphone

UPDRAFT is controlled by blowing into the phone, so the app asks for the RECORD_AUDIO permission. Here is exactly what happens with it:

  • The microphone is open only while you fly: during breath calibration, the tutorial, the moment before a run starts, the countdown, the run itself and the short resume after a pause. It is closed in menus, during ads and whenever the app is in the background.
  • The sound is turned into a few numbers on the device, many times per second: how strong the blow is, how confident the detector is, whether a Gust was detected and how good the input quality is. Only these numbers are used; the audio itself is discarded immediately.
  • No audio is ever written to storage or logs, and nothing about your voice or breath is sent anywhere. We never ask players for audio samples.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android settings. The game remains playable without it in Touch Mode (lift by holding the screen).

Motion sensors

Steering uses the phone’s rotation or gravity sensors to measure how far you tilt it. The angle is processed on the device while you play and is not stored or transmitted. If your phone has no suitable sensor, you steer by swiping instead.

Ads & your choices

  • UPDRAFT shows rewarded ads only — to revive a run, to double a reward, for the Daily Bonus, for free Stardust in the Shop, to try a style for one flight or to reroll one more of tonight's stars. Every one of them is your choice, and there are at most 20 rewarded ads a day. There are no banners and no forced ads between runs.
  • The ads SDK (Google AdMob) loads an ad in the background so it is ready if you want it; nothing plays unless you choose it. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (Google’s G rating).
  • UPDRAFT is for a general audience aged 13 and over. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we treat every player as possibly under the age of digital consent, so ads there are never personalised and the game shows no consent form.
  • Elsewhere, you can limit ad personalisation in Android settings (Privacy → Ads). Where a privacy message applies in your region, Settings → Privacy options in the game lets you change your choices at any time.

Purchases

Cosmetic items (skins and trails, one by one or in packs) and early flyer unlocks are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and shares only the order details we need to deliver the purchase. Purchases are restored automatically on the same Google account, or with Restore purchases in the Shop or in Settings. Pre-registration rewards (Founder Feather) are delivered the same way. Styles never change physics or fairness, and every flyer bought early can also be unlocked by playing.

In-app items are digital content delivered immediately; Google Play handles payment and refunds, and Google Commerce Limited is the seller. Because the items are delivered at once, under Google Play’s terms the statutory right of withdrawal ends when you confirm the purchase. See also the terms of use.

Your data on your device & backups

  • Your progress — records, unlocks, Stardust, settings, calibration — is stored on your phone.
  • Android may back it up with Android Auto Backup to your Google Drive, encrypted by Google, so that it comes back after a reinstall. Breath calibration, tilt and quality settings are excluded because they belong to one device. Your ad privacy choices are excluded too, so they are never restored from a backup on another device. You can turn backups off in Android settings.
  • Settings → Privacy → Delete my data erases your progress and scores on the device. Purchases stay restorable through Google Play. It does not change your ad privacy choices (Privacy options, where shown) or your Google advertising ID (Android settings).
  • Share card: when you share or save a picture of your flight or your sky, it is made on your phone and shows your flight (altitude, flyer, world) or your stars: no name, no account, no tracking link. The text that goes with it is “How high can you go?” and a plain link to UPDRAFT on Google Play, without tracking parameters. It goes only where you send it.

If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data (for example by e-mail), write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export the data we hold and to restrict its processing, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21). Where you have given consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier processing; for ads, use Privacy options in the game where they are shown.

Because the app sends us nothing, we usually hold only e-mails you sent us and purchase order records. For data held by Google, use Google’s tools: Privacy options in the game, Android Settings → Google → Ads, and myaccount.google.com.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. In Slovenia that is the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (Informacijski pooblaščenec), Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, www.ip-rs.si.

How long we keep data

  • Purchase records: as long as tax law requires.
  • Crash reports: as long as Google Play Console keeps them.

We hold no other data.

Security

Game data stays in the app’s private storage on your phone. Google’s services transmit data encrypted (TLS).

This website

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub, Inc. (USA) is the host. GitHub may record visitor IP addresses in its server logs for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. We add no cookies, no analytics and no tracking of our own.

  • The breath demo on the home page can use your microphone only if you tap the microphone button and allow it. The sound is analysed inside your browser and is never recorded or uploaded. It stops when you leave the demo.

Mikrofon

UPDRAFT krmiliš s pihanjem v telefon, zato aplikacija zahteva dovoljenje RECORD_AUDIO. Točno tako ga uporabljamo:

  • Mikrofon je odprt samo med letom: med umerjanjem diha, v vadnici, tik pred začetkom vožnje, med odštevanjem, med vožnjo in med kratkim nadaljevanjem po premoru. Zaprt je v menijih, med oglasi in vedno, ko je aplikacija v ozadju.
